# Embryo Equation Solver

Embryo Equation Solver is Embryo-Zero's fork of [FEniCS DOLFINx](https://github.com/FEniCS/dolfinx) (74f010a, commit 74f010a). It does solves physics equations (finite elements).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING, COPYING.LESSER) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
